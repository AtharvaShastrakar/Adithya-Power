CONTROLLED DEWATERING & FIRING

Dry-Out Process

Our dry-out process is crucial to removing residual moisture from the refractory materials, preventing risks like thermal shock and cracking. We utilize advanced equipment and techniques to ensure effective and controlled drying.

Dry-Out Services Include:

PHASE 01

Customized Heating Schedules

Tailored heating plans specific to each refractory material and application.

Calculated soak periods at 110°C, 350°C, and 550°C for free & crystal water release.
PHASE 02

Temperature Monitoring

Advanced sensors and controls for precise temperature management during drying.

Real-time multi-point thermocouple telemetry logging delta-T across furnace walls.
PHASE 03

Post-Dry-Out Inspection

Thorough inspections ensure the refractories are fully ready for operation.

Visual and acoustic sounding checks certifying zero microcracks or surface spalls.

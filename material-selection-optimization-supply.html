STRUCTURED ASSESSMENT

Material Selection Process

Our three-tier evaluation methodology matches metallurgical demands with the highest performance refractory materials:

STEP 01

1. Operational Assessment

Understanding the key factors like temperature ranges, chemical exposure, mechanical loads, and thermal cycling.

Profiling peak operating heats, hot gas erosiveness, and furnace atmosphere.
STEP 02

2. Material Analysis

Choosing materials that offer the best thermal, chemical, and mechanical properties for the task.

Screening Al₂O₃, MgO, SiC, and ZrO₂ ratios to resist specific chemical attacks.
STEP 03

3. Cost Optimization

Ensuring that performance requirements are met while maintaining cost-effectiveness.

Balancing high-grade hot-face materials with economical backup insulation.

Recommending materials that minimize heat loss and enhance energy efficiency.

By strategically combining low-thermal-conductivity microporous boards and ceramic fiber blankets behind dense refractory faces, we drastically slash furnace skin temperatures and fuel burn.

Cold-Face Temperature Drop

Lowering exterior shell temperature by up to 60°C, protecting plant personnel and structural steel.

Direct Fuel Savings

Reduces fuel gas and electrical power consumption in rotary kilns, ladles, and heat-treating furnaces.

Low Heat Storage

Enables faster heating and cooling turnarounds for batch processes and intermittent cycling.

•

Prioritizing materials with longer service lives to reduce maintenance costs.

Selecting refractory bricks and castables with low open porosity, superior cold crushing strength (CCS), and synthetic spinel or silicon carbide additives to prevent premature erosion.

High Slag Resistance

Resin-bonded Mag-Carbon and direct-bonded Mag-Chrome preventing deep chemical flux penetration.

Thermal Shock Resilience

High-purity mullite and alumina-spinel formulations eliminating surface flaking and structural cracking.

Reduced Relining Cycles

Doubles furnace campaign running hours, substantially reducing annual refractory maintenance budgets.

•

Tailoring materials to specific processes, ensuring the highest level of compatibility.

Customized formulations for alkali-bypass ducts, aluminium reverberatory non-wetting hearths, fluidized catalytic cracking risers, and hazardous waste incinerators.

Alkali-Resistant Formulations

Preventing alkali burst and ring formation in modern precalciner cement kilns.

Anti-Corundum Barriers

Acid-Proof Polymer Mortars
